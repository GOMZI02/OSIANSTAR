❋ About Us ❋

Founded in 1978 in Erode, Tamil Nadu, 'Oswal' initially operated under the name Oswal Textile Mills, quickly becoming a prominent seller of cotton fabrics by 1983. With the burgeoning demand for polyester fabrics, the company transitioned to Surat, Gujarat, in 1986, garnering widespread acclaim for its innovative polyester textiles.

In 2010, Oswal Fibrotex Mills India Pvt Ltd was established, marking our expansion into numerous African countries. Building on this success, in 2016, we introduced 'Osian Star Exports LLP', extending our business reach to Turkey, Egypt, Tunisia, Algeria, Jordan, Ethiopia, and Sudan. Renowned as 'Osian Star', our registered trademark, we have excelled in exporting a diverse range of embroidery fabrics, including schiffli and multy, solidifying our reputation as a leader in the textile industry.

Our vision is to be a global leader in sustainable business practices and environmental commitment to innovation & empowerment,Throughgrowth, Our Vision & Mission and innovation, we aspire to create a brighter, more sustainable future for our organization, our stakeholders, and the world at large. We envision a world where businesses prioritize environmental stewardship and social responsibility, leading to a thriving ecosystem of sustainable development and progress.

Our mission is to offer sustainable business solutions and develop innovative, environmentally friendly ideas that empower individuals within our organization and drive growth in society. We aim to foster a culture of creativity and collaboration to design and produce cutting-edge products that meet the needs of today while preserving the planet for future generations.

As a distinguished presence in the textile industry, we specialize in manufacturing and exporting a wide array of high-quality premium fabrics and laces. Leveraging years of expertise and a formidable market position, we've diversified our offerings to include a garment division, dedicated to meeting the sophisticated preferences and discerning tastes of customers across Japan, Scandinavia, Australia, and South America.
