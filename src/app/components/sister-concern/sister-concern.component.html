The company is led by Mr. Shreyans Ajitsingh Jain, a visionary entrepreneur and Managing Director, whose commitment to excellence and global business acumen continues to drive the growth and diversification of the group.

Headquartered in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates, Oswal Global Agro FZ LLC is a dynamic trading company specializing in the import and export of premium raw materials for the textile and fabric industries. As a strategic extension of the Osian Star legacy, the company strengthens our global reach by serving as a reliable partner for sourcing and trading natural fibers and raw cotton across international markets. With a strong focus on quality, efficiency, and seamless global trade, Oswal Global Agro FZ LLC plays a crucial role in bridging the demand-supply gap for textile raw materials in both local and international markets.

Core Business Areas:

◆ Trading of raw cotton, combed cotton, and natural fibers.

◆ Import and export of essential raw materials for fabric and textile production.

◆ Catering to markets across the Middle East, Asia, and Europe.
